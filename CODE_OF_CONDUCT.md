# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in CycleIQ a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or health condition.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people sharing their own health experiences
- Respecting different viewpoints, experiences and bodies
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the people who use the app

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Dismissing or mocking anyone's symptoms, diagnosis, body or health choices
- Giving medical advice as though it were authoritative, or pressuring anyone about their health
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, **including any health information**, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Health information

Never post real health data (yours or anyone else's) in issues, pull requests, screenshots or test fixtures. Use made-up sample data. Maintainers will edit or remove content that contains it.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting form ("Report a vulnerability" under the repository's **Security** tab), marking the report as a conduct issue, or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction.** A private, written warning explaining the problem, and possibly a request for a public apology.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour; no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards or disparagement of individuals.
