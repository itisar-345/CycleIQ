# User Research Guide — PCOS & Endometriosis Sessions

For the checklist items *"User research sessions with PCOS community members"* and *"… endometriosis community members"*. Sessions must be run by people; this guide makes them consistent and comparable.

## Goals

1. Can people complete onboarding and a daily log without help?
2. Do condition-specific questions feel relevant, respectful and complete?
3. Are predictions, confidence and insights understood correctly (no over-trust)?
4. Which voice (chill / classic) do participants prefer, and does either feel off-putting?
5. Do safety prompts feel caring rather than alarming?

## Recruitment

- 5–8 participants per condition (PCOS, endometriosis); mix of ages (18–45), diagnosed and suspected, iOS and Android.
- Recruit via patient communities / support groups with the group's permission.
- Exclude anyone currently in crisis; have support resources ready (see the Support screen).
- Informed consent form; compensation; participants may stop at any time.
- **Use a fresh QA build with demo data only.** Never ask participants to enter their real health data during a session, and never record their screens with personal data.

## Session plan (45 min)

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 5 min | Intro, consent, "we're testing the app, not you" |
| 10 min | Onboarding on a test phone, thinking aloud |
| 10 min | Tasks: log today; find a past day in Calendar; switch to flare mode (endo) / log PCOS symptoms |
| 10 min | Comprehension: "What does this prediction tell you? How sure is it? What would you do with this insight?" |
| 5 min | Show both voices side by side — preference and why |
| 5 min | Wrap-up: missing questions/symptoms, anything uncomfortable |

## Questions to ask

- "Was there any question you didn't understand or didn't want to answer?"
- "Is anything you track for your PCOS/endo missing?"
- "If you saw this alert [show red-flag / safeguarding mock], how would it make you feel? What would you do?"
- "Would you trust this app with your data? Why / why not?"
- "Would you share the doctor report with your clinician?"
